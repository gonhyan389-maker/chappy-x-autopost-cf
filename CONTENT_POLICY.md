## Content mix
### 3 / day — ChatGPT tips
- Immediately usable copy-paste prompts
- Strong but truthful hook such as “これを入れると回答が変わる”
- Do not promise guaranteed or impossible outcomes
- Make the actual prompt visible in the post
- Favor practical use: decision making, writing, research, task management, SNS, learning

### 3 / day — AI COACH VIDEO
- Product explanation
- Realistic use cases
- How to use / workflow
- Development story / tests
- CTA: https://ai-coach-app.com/video/
- Avoid pretending there are results or functions that have not been verified

## Safety

- Reiya must explicitly approve a post before scheduling.

- No auto-follow, mass reply, mass DM or engagement spam.
